# Mode: Social Post

Hebrew Facebook / English LinkedIn posts about research findings.

**Trigger keywords**: "Facebook post", "LinkedIn post", "write a post about", "פוסט לפייסבוק", "כתוב פוסט", "פוסט על הממצאים"

---

## Core Principle

**This is STORYTELLING, not a data summary.** Every post tells a narrative: what we knew, what was missing, what we did, what we found, and why it matters. Data serves the story, not the other way around.

---

## Reference Material

Keep one or two of YOUR OWN best past posts as canonical examples (e.g., in your reference bank),
and read them before writing a new post to internalize the rhythm and voice. The bracketed slots
below show the target *rhythm* — fill them with your own topic and numbers. (The illustrative
Hebrew is generic scaffolding, not a real finding.)

---

## Mandatory Structure (12 sections, in order)

### 1. Hook Questions
Multiple direct questions. Can open with a quantitative hook before a personal one.
> [שאלה כמותית: כמה אנשים עושים X?] [שאלה אישית: מי הם האנשים שעושים X כשקשה להם?]

### 2. Context: Prior Work
First person plural. Narrative arc through previous findings.
> בשנים האחרונות הראינו ש[...], טענו ש[...], וניסינו להבין [...].

Strong verbs: "הראינו" (we showed), "טענו" (we argued), not "חקרנו" (we researched).

### 3. The Gap
Short. Punchy. Its own paragraph.
> אבל לאורך הדרך היה חסר לנו משהו מהותי. [מה חסר — בשתי-שלוש מילים.]

### 4. The Turn
One line. Stands alone.
> אז הלכנו לבדוק.

### 5. Method: Who, How, What
- Team members WITH academic titles (ד"ר, פרופ')
- First person: "ד"ר X, ד"ר Y ואני"
- Sample size, screening methodology
- "סיננו בקפידה, שיערנו השערות מראש" (rigor language)
- Research questions as actual questions the team asked

### 6. Transition to Findings
Modest, inviting. Its own line:
> והנה קצת ממה שמצאנו עד כה:

### 7. Findings: Data Woven into Narrative
**Longest section. Must FLOW as connected prose, not bullet points.**

Each finding builds on the previous:
1. Lead finding with natural language + number: "כמעט שליש מהמשתתפים, [XX%]"
2. International or benchmark comparison woven in (not separate)
3. "Who" paragraph: age, gender, and other characteristics, all flowing together
4. "Why" paragraph: reasons as flowing text with embedded numbers
5. Key reframing stat: a single number that flips the obvious reading
6. Outcome numbers

Use "כלומר" to translate data into plain meaning.
Use questions to transition: "ומי האנשים ש...?" "ולמה דווקא [X]?"

### 8. Key Insight
Reframe data into human truth. Challenge the obvious reading.
> [משפט שהופך את הפרשנות המתבקשת — האנשים לא עושים X כי "משהו לא בסדר"; הם עושים X כי [...].]

### 9. Narrative Summary
One sentence capturing the recurring pattern/tension.

### 10. Paper Status
Brief. Factual. Link in comments (Facebook) or inline (LinkedIn).

### 11. What's Next + Closing
Tease future work, then "no longer X, the question is Y" formula:
> השאלה היא כבר לא "[השאלה הישנה]". השאלה היא [the real question].

### 12. Tags
- Team members by name
- Institutional page
- 3-4 professional communities
- NO hashtags on Facebook. LinkedIn: 3-5 hashtags at bottom only.

## Voice Rules

### DO
- Hebrew for Facebook, English for LinkedIn (same structure and voice)
- First person: "ואני", "הלכנו", "מצאנו"
- Full flowing paragraphs. Data embedded in sentences.
- Short sentences for impact. Fragments for drama.
- Numbers integrated naturally: "כמעט שליש מהמשתתפים, [XX%]"
- Academic titles for team: ד"ר, פרופ'
- Explain every jargon
- Empathy for people in data
- Balance: benefit AND risk
- Colloquial punch lines
- End with a question, never a conclusion

### DO NOT
- NEVER reduce to bullet points
- No emojis
- No hashtags on Facebook
- No "proud to announce" / "excited to share"
- No academic dryness
- No hedging
- No alarmism or cheerleading

## Input Required

Before writing:
1. **Topic/paper** — which findings to present
2. **Key numbers** — data points (or path to R output / Results.md)
3. **Team members** — who to credit, with academic titles
4. **Prior work** — what to reference as context
5. **Upcoming work** — what to tease

## Mode-Specific Anti-AI

- No emojis
