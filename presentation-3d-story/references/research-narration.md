# Research narration branch

Read this branch for scholarly, scientific, clinical, or other evidence-heavy presentations. Match the audit to the requested work: a local wording repair does not require reopening every manuscript.

## Writer and audience preparation

Use the available writer agent for substantive research prose. Load its canonical `AGENT.md` and follow the complete mandatory sequence: writer memory and feedback, core lessons, writing rules, voice, author context, relevant language rules, and presentation/revision mode. Load manuscript and citation rules where the task requires them. On the configured Elad workstation, the canonical entrypoint is `C:/Users/user/.claude/agents/writer/AGENT.md`; use the installed location elsewhere.

Build the argument map before drafting. Review logic, evidence, narrative continuity, paragraph purpose, voice, citations, and language. Reuse a stack already read in the current context; do not recreate it from memory. If a required writing dependency is unavailable, report the limit and continue source mapping or preserve verified source wording instead of claiming the full review occurred.

Apply the available `aitom` skill to the actual audience. Before a result, identify the construct and question. Explain what was compared and why it matters. Automated language lint cannot establish the quality of a language it was not designed to assess; combine it with manual review.

## Establish authority, then narrate

Maintain a source ledger with location, version/date, verification status, and the reason a file is authoritative. The deck supplies the narrative order; the designated authoritative analysis supplies numbers; the actual methods and questionnaire definitions establish meaning. A newer filename alone does not resolve a conflict. Read the conflicting sources or mark the claim unresolved.

For each displayed fact, verify:

- The population, analysis sample, item-specific denominator, and unit counted: people, records, messages, events, or another unit.
- The actual question/construct, reference period, response options, and whether multiple selections were allowed.
- The estimate, measurement unit, model, uncertainty, and rounding from available precision.
- The design and permitted inference. Separate self-reported experience from observed behavior or demonstrated effectiveness.
- Publication status independently from evidence type. A theoretical article remains theoretical after publication; an unpublished analysis may contain empirical results.

Do not infer treatment cessation from a changed survey answer, a global population rate from selected samples, or independent replications from papers sharing data. Claims about mechanisms, within-person change, and causal effects require supporting designs and analyses.

## Make statistics intelligible

Lead with the substantive finding. Define the variables before displaying coefficients. Keep effect units visible in technical details; coefficients using different units are not a valid visual ranking of importance.

Odds ratios are not percentage-point changes or proportional probability increases. A conditional effect must retain its moderator condition in the main claim; explain modeled evaluation points in details. Do not turn a coefficient into an absolute trajectory without a verified prediction model.

Use the uncertainty the source provides. Never fabricate a missing confidence interval or silently infer one from rounded figures. A request for additional calculation is a separate, documented analytical step.

Choose the displayed scope for the argument without presenting an unsupported result as established. If the user requests that a nonsignificant result be omitted, document that focus decision and retain the complete source analysis; preserve any qualification needed to interpret the displayed finding. Do not impose omission as a general rule.

## Theory, recommendations, and illustrations

Use visible language appropriate to the evidence: reported pattern, observed association, experimental result, proposed explanation, candidate function, or recommendation. Give theory enough substance to be understood, while distinguishing it from a direct test.

A metaphor represents a proposed role or process; it does not establish efficacy. State whether paired concepts can overlap, coexist, or change by context when that distinction matters. Do not map every relational function exclusively to people or every instrumental function exclusively to technology unless the actual source supports that claim.

A fictional example may show what a construct means in ordinary life. It cannot serve as a participant quotation, explain a statistical percentage, or supply an untested chain from exposure through harm or benefit to recovery. Ground themes in authorized aggregates or published syntheses, not individual records.

Read cited primary sources when their content is needed and not already provided. Record exactly what each contributes and its population/tool/design boundary. Do not add citations merely because their authors or titles sound relevant.

## Handoff checks

Keep the same claim boundaries in the main panel, oral notes, source drawer, article card, caption, enlarged graph, navigation label, and spatial cue. Preserve source-fact identifiers when the display becomes less numerical. Separate unresolved facts from verified ones so implementation cannot accidentally render a null as zero or an unknown status as confirmed.
